# Bad Times AI – Photo → Dance Video

Turn **one photo** into a short Bad Times dance / argue video. Solo, group, or pet. Pay once — no subscription.

**Live app:** https://badtimesai.dev

## What it does

- Upload one portrait or group photo
- Pick a Bad Times dance template (about 13–15 seconds)
- Get a vertical 480p MP4 with the template soundtrack
- From **$7.99** per video · credit packs available · credits never expire

## Who it's for

- People who want the viral Bad Times meme with their own face
- Pet owners (yes, pets dance too)
- Group photos — everyone in the shot can dance together

## Quick start

1. Open https://badtimesai.dev
2. Choose a template
3. Upload a clear photo (JPG / PNG / WebP, ≤10 MB)
4. Pay once and download the MP4
