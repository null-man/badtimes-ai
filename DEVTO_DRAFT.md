# How I turn one selfie into a Bad Times AI dance video (no subscription)

The Bad Times meme is everywhere: head in hands, palm out, then straight into the camera on the beat. I wanted that clip with *my* face — and my cat's — without another monthly AI subscription.

So I shipped **[Bad Times AI](https://badtimesai.dev)**: one photo in, ~15s vertical MP4 out, pay once.

## What you need

- One clear photo (JPG / PNG / WebP, ≤10 MB)
- Face(s) visible — solo, group, or pet
- About a minute and a one-time payment (from $7.99)

## Steps

1. Open [badtimesai.dev](https://badtimesai.dev)
2. Preview the dance templates and pick your moves
3. Upload the photo (group photos make everyone dance)
4. Pay once → wait for the render → download the portrait MP4

Credits never expire if you buy a pack. Confirmed failed generations can be retried.

## Photo tips that actually matter

- Face sharp and forward, eyes visible
- Even light (harsh shadows break lipsync / motion)
- Empty hands if you want clean gestures
- One clear subject works best; crop busy group shots

## Why pay-once

Most meme tools nudge you into weekly subs. For a trend that lasts weeks, not years, one-time packs felt more honest: try a selfie, make a pet version, stop.
